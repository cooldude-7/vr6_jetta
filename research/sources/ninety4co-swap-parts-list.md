# Ninety4co "MK6 GTI 3.6L BLV Swap Parts List" (reference copy)

Source: Google Doc linked in every episode of *The VR6 MK6 Swap* (Ninety4co, host Choby / @chobester).
https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lUm4Pee3PA9zaG5dA_x5aLbu-WO3YJs_4todYXtXFYo/edit
Doc last updated **6/27/2024**. Copied 2026-10-08 and reformatted into tables; wording in the Notes column is his, lightly trimmed.

His own caveat, verbatim in spirit: *this is not an end-all-be-all list.* It is what he used/discovered for **his** car: a **2010 GTI, CCTA 2.0T, 6-speed manual, high-line**. Some parts came with his engine purchase and are not listed.

Our car is a **Mk6 Jetta**, not a Mk6 GTI. Every "stock mk6" line below means *stock Mk6 Golf/GTI* and must be re-verified for the Jetta. See `research/ninety4co-vr6-mk6-swap-series.md`, section "What transfers to a Mk6 Jetta".

## Drivetrain, mounts, electrical, fuel, exhaust

## Cooling

He used a **Gray Fabrication 3.6L swirl pot**, which replaces the OEM coolant bottle and deletes several hoses. If you keep an OEM-style system, the hoses are the same as a **2018 Passat GT**; look them up on parts.vw.com.
