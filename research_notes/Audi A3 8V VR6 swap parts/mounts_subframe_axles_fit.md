# Mechanical fit: VW Atlas 3.6 VR6 (EA390) into a 2015–2020 Audi A3/S3/RS3 8V (MQB): mounts, dogbone, subframe, axles, quattro driveline, bay/hood, suspension/brakes

Research date: 2026-10-08. Scope: North American cars only. Donor: 2018–2023 VW Atlas / Atlas Cross Sport 3.6 (EA390, 8-speed Aisin automatic). Recipient: 2015–2020 Audi A3 / S3 / RS3 8V sedan, ideally quattro. PQ35 findings from the Mk6 Jetta notes (`../Mk6 Jetta CDVC VR6 swap parts/mounts_subframe_axles_fit.md`) are referenced, not repeated.

Labels: **VERIFIED** = factory parts-catalog fitment (oemwolf.com, ETKA-derived), factory/OEM press document, or a vendor product page/JSON I opened. **REPORTED** = forum/build/press article, or a retailer or dealer listing seen only as a search-engine excerpt. **INFERRED** = my reasoning.

Method notes (important for weighting): (1) oemwolf.com still works via curl but only shows **VW-brand fitment up to MY2015**; it returns nothing for Audi 8V-only numbers (8V0…) or Atlas numbers (3QF…/3QD…), so A3 and Atlas fitment comes mainly from vendor pages: UroTuning product JSON (fitment tags by chassis/engine/years), FCP Euro category pages (fetched with WebFetch), BFI and 034Motorsport pages. (2) fcpeuro, ecstuning, parts.audiusa, audiusaparts, 7zap and partsouq return HTTP 403 to curl; vwpartsvortex.com is now a spam domain. (3) Vendor fitment says which cars a part is **sold for**, not every chassis it would physically bolt to. (4) Platform naming: the brief calls the A3 "MQB A1" and the Atlas "MQB A2/MQB37W"; Wikipedia's variant list was garbled in the fetch, so I treat both simply as MQB (first generation) and do not rely on the sub-codes.

## Summary: which OEM parts would make an Atlas VR6 bolt into an A3 8V, and where fabrication is unavoidable

### Takeaway
No complete OEM path exists, because VW never put a VR6 in an MQB compact car (Golf 7/A3 8V/TT 8S). But the MQB parts show that the A3 and the Atlas share the right frame-rail mount footprint, the front-subframe bolting, and the prop-shaft front flex-disc. So the most plausible near-bolt-in **engine side** is the donor's own right mount **3QF 199 262 G/H** with the VR6 block console **022 199 354 T**, bolted to the A3's right rail (INFERRED, not yet test-fitted by anyone on record). The **gearbox side** decides everything else. If you keep an MQB DSG behind the VR6 (as HPA did in Golf Rs and HGP did with an RS3 gearbox), the A3/S3/RS3 trans mount, dogbone, PTU, prop shaft and Haldex rear stay stock. If you use the Atlas's 8-speed Aisin, its left mount (**3QF 199 555 C**) does not use the battery-tray brackets the A3 uses, there is no A3 bracket for it, the A3 axles/PTU/prop-shaft pairing is lost, and fabrication plus custom axles are needed.

**Fabrication / no-OEM-solution points (flagged):**
1. **No OEM VR6 mount was ever built for an A3/Golf 7/TT 8S rail.** The candidate (3QF 199 262 G/H + 022 199 354 T) is an inference from shared MQB fitment, not a documented fit. Expect to verify the bolt pattern and, if it differs, fabricate an adapter plate. INFERRED.
2. **Atlas 8-speed in an A3:** the left mount 3QF 199 555 C uses a different body attachment from the A3's battery-tray-bracket mount, and no A3 bracket exists for it, so **fabricate a bracket**. The Atlas PTU/axle geometry also differs from the A3's (see 5). REPORTED (BFI) + INFERRED.
3. **Axles with the Atlas gearbox:** Atlas inner joints + A3 outer joints/lengths, so **custom axles**. INFERRED.
4. **PTU / prop-shaft alignment with an Atlas PTU:** no source places the Atlas PTU output relative to the A3 tunnel. The flex disc is common, but **prop-shaft length/centre-bearing work may be needed**. INFERRED.
5. **Oil pan / subframe / steering rack and firewall/hood clearances:** no measurements or Atlas sump/subframe numbers found, so **unknown; budget for trial fit, possible sump or exhaust-manifold/downpipe fabrication**. Gap.
6. **Engine-side console for a DSG build:** the 022 199 354 T console carries the mount; nothing extra is needed if the 3QF mount fits. If a DQ-series DSG is used, the gearbox-side mount bosses are the DSG's own, so the A3 mounts apply. INFERRED.

### Inferences
- INFERRED: The cheapest "mostly OEM" architecture is EA390 + MQB DSG (DQ250 0D9 from an S3/A3 quattro, DQ381, or RS3 DQ500) using the A3/S3/RS3 left mount, dogbone, PTU, axles, prop shaft and 0CQ rear. Precedent: HPA's VR6 Golf R/Alltrack and HGP's 3.6 Golf R with an RS3 gearbox (Q1, Q4). Bellhousing/flywheel compatibility is the transmission researcher's question.
- INFERRED: Using the Atlas 8-speed adds three fabrication items (left-mount bracket, axles, PTU/prop alignment) and is the less bolt-in route, even though it keeps the donor's engine/gearbox calibration together.

### Gaps
- No physical test-fit, photo or measurement of an Atlas 3QF mount on an A3/Golf 7 rail was found. The only MQB-compact VR6 builds found (HPA, HGP, Team Nerdo) do not disclose their mounts.
- Exact factory suffixes by VIN for a given A3 (e.g. 5Q0 199 262 BK vs BE/BF/BH; 5Q0 199 555 T vs BK) were not confirmable: dealer catalogs returned 403, and oemwolf has no Audi data.

## Q1. Engine (right) mount: A3 8V numbers by engine, Atlas 3.6 mount and block bracket, shared MQB rail interface, any OEM VR6 bracket on an MQB compact rail

### Takeaway
A3/S3 8V (EA888 Gen3 1.8T/2.0T) cars use the **5Q0 199 262** MQB mount family (UroTuning's genuine listing is **5Q0 199 262 BK**). The RS3 8V uses its own **8V0 199 262 B**. The Atlas 3.6 uses **3QF 199 262 G / H** on the body side and the VR6 block console **022 199 354 T**, the same console family VW fitted to PQ35/PQ46 VR6 cars. CTS sells one MQB 4-cylinder mount as a direct bolt-on for both the A3 8V and the Atlas 2.0T. So the right frame-rail mount pocket appears common to the A3 and the Atlas, which makes 3QF 199 262 G/H + 022 199 354 T the leading candidate for a VR6 on the A3 rail (INFERRED). No OEM part was ever fitted that way.

### Inferences
- INFERRED: The MQB right mount is a two-interface part: the body side bolts to the frame-rail pocket, and the arm bolts to an engine-specific console (06K… on EA888, 022 199 354 on VR6). CTS's single mount for both the A3 8V and the Atlas 2.0T implies the Atlas rail pocket matches the A3's. The Atlas 3.6 mount 3QF 199 262 G/H bolts into that same Atlas pocket, so it should bolt into the A3 pocket and accept the EA390's 022 199 354 T console. This is the only OEM "VR6 bracket on an MQB rail" combination that exists, and it has never been documented in an A3/Golf 7.
- INFERRED: Because the 022 199 354 S/T console spans the Mk5 R32, Eos 3.2, NMS Passat 3.6 and Atlas, the EA390 keeps the same block-to-console interface across PQ and MQB. The platform-specific piece is the body-side mount (1K0 199 262 AR on PQ35, 3QF 199 262 G/H on MQB).
- INFERRED: The RS3 8V shows that an A3 8V rail can carry a non-EA888 engine on a different mount (8V0 199 262 B). It is a factory precedent for a heavier, longer engine on the same rail, not for a VR6.
- INFERRED: The engine's height and fore/aft position on the 3QF mount follow the Atlas's EA390 installation. If MQB "engine positioning" is truly shared, crank and output-flange positions land where the A3's drivetrain expects them. This is the key assumption to verify on a test fit.

### Gaps
- The bolt count/spacing of the 3QF 199 262 G/H body flange vs 5Q0 199 262 BK was not found in any drawing or listing. Whether G→H is a supersession or an engine/drive split is unknown.
- What 5Q0 199 517 D/E and 518 D/E ("support") are in the illustration (rail brackets vs carrier brackets) is unclear.
- The A3 8V's exact Gen3 console number was not found (06K 199 207 A is confirmed only for PQ35 and the Atlas 2.0T per FCP).

## Q2. Transmission (left) mount and pendulum/dogbone: A3/S3/RS3 vs Atlas; which gearbox-side brackets line up

### Takeaway
A3/S3 8V use **5Q0 199 555 T / BK** (DQ250/MQ250/350, and BFI says the same mount serves 2018+ 7-speed DSG S3/GTI/R). The RS3 uses **8V0 199 555 A**. The Atlas uses **3QF 199 555 C**, which BFI says is "a bit different than all the other MQB mounts" and "doesn't require the battery tray brackets that most other MQB cars use". So the Atlas 8-speed's left mount has **no A3 attachment point (fabrication)**, while any MQB DSG keeps the A3/S3/RS3 mount. Dogbones: A3/S3 **5Q0 199 855 AD / J**; TT-RS (and Tiguan 8-speed) **5Q0 199 855 AH**; Atlas **3QD 199 855**.

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Engine mass alone: EA390 ~188 kg (weakly sourced) vs the US RS3's aluminium 2.5 TFSI ≈ 157 kg (Audi's 183 kg cast-iron figure minus 26 kg). A VR6 A3 would carry roughly 30 kg more than an RS3 on the front axle before gearbox differences, i.e. roughly 920–950 kg front at RS3-like curb weight. That is within the 8Y RS3's 1,105 kg rating but heavier than any 8V spring class except the RS3's. Use RS3 8V front springs/dampers (or coilovers valved for RS3 weight) and RS3 front brakes (370 mm discs + RS3 calipers + RS3 knuckle/carrier, 18-in minimum wheels).
- INFERRED: Because the strut-top and subframe interfaces are common across A3/S3/RS3/Golf R (034 shock mount, Tyrolsport/Verkline subframes), RS3 suspension and brake parts are the OEM bolt-on path. Caliper-carrier/knuckle compatibility for the RS3 brake on an A3 knuckle needs checking.
