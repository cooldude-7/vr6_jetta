## The car

- **Recipient:** 2017 Audi S3 8V quattro, previously a stage-3 2.0T drag car (Shop tour 5:00; Ep 1 0:00).
- **Engine:** brand-new 3.6 VR6 long block bought from Europe, engine code **BWS** (the 300 PS Passat R36 / CC R36 engine, "a different crankshaft, different pistons" from the NA 3.6) (Ep 1 2:01–3:00; Shop tour 5:00). Built for boost: block and head machined for 11 mm head studs (stock 9 mm bolts), upgraded "X-beam" rods (had to be machined to the piston taper), stock pistons with opened ring gaps, main-cap girdle, head spacer plate, stock valvetrain with 1 mm spring shims, Garrett G42-1200 turbo, IE billet spin-on oil filter housing, port + direct injection (12 injectors), RS3 throttle body (Ep 1 3:00–4:00; Ep 2 2:01; Shop tour 5:00).
- **Gearbox: RS3/TT RS DQ500 7-speed DSG, imported used from Europe. "We had the bell housing machined to mate up to the VR6, cuz typically they don't mate to the VR6, they're made for the 2.5 L."** Stock DQ500 clutches retained at first. A **VR6 flywheel** was ordered (took a month to arrive) and clearanced because the main girdle hit it (Ep 2 0:00–1:00).
- **AWD:** retained. DQ500 with its bevel box (PTU), prop shaft, and Haldex rear diff (racing clutches added in the rear diff). Under hard launches DSG fluid and bevel-box oil blow out of the breathers; fixed with CTS catch cans and a TSB reseal of the bevel-box case seam (Prep video 1:00–5:00, 7:13).
- **Mounts:**
  - Transmission side: **RS3 mount** ("both 8V Audi, it mounts to the frame rail the same and the RS3 mount works with the DQ500").
  - Engine side: **Atlas 3.6 VR6 mount**. "Older VR6 mounts don't mount to the MQB frame that well … because they're pretty much the same chassis style it works, it puts the engine in the right spot. It's a bit teeny tiny so I don't know how well that will work with a lot of power."
  - Dogbone: **RS3-style**; "the 2 L one was just too big and actually didn't reach far enough to get to the bottom of the transmission" (Ep 2 2:01–3:00).
- **Front end and cooling:** CTS RS3 race intercooler and crash bar needed the **RS3 radiator support** (RS3 intercoolers mount outside the rad support). Kept the **S3 radiator**. The 3.6 was a bare long block, so the S3's own cooling hoses were "cut up and used", working from cooling diagrams because coolant flow differs between the engines (Ep 2 3:00–4:01; Ep 3 0:00–1:01). Stainless coolant-return line to the bottle because of manifold heat; phenolic intake spacer for heat soak (Ep 3 8:04–9:03).
- **Exhaust:** hood-exit turbo exhaust and wastegate; second wastegate added on the turbine housing (Ep 3 2:03; Shop tour 6:00).
- **Battery:** relocated to the trunk with a junction box up front, "like an RS3 or an R32". "It doesn't fit in the engine bay anymore" (Ep 3 3:05).
- **ECU: a factory VW ECU out of a VW Atlas 3.6 (Bosch MED17), with TuneZilla's own software patches** for multiport injection and boost control; the S3's Simos 18.10 was removed. No standalone. "Rewiring the ECU, repinning the ECU took a bit of time … wiring diagrams for ECUs are all over the place … a few weeks just to get the wiring in and get the car turning on … lots of communication errors just getting certain modules to talk to each other properly … in the end it ended up working" (Ep 3 3:05–5:03; Shop tour 6:00).
- **Fuel:** Walbro 535 at first; then a ProTex MQB AWD pump module (keeps factory returnless regulator location), then a large brushless pump with its own controller, the ProTex pump kept as a saddle-tank transfer pump on an **RS3 brushless pump controller**; PTFE lines, ethanol sensor (Ep 3 5:03–7:04).
- **Starting:** first start took "multiple tries", partly an old battery that couldn't spin the VR6 (Ep 3 7:04–8:04).
- **Timeline:** planned ~2 years; teardown winter 2023–24; engine in by June 2024; first start by March 2025; racing 2025 season. "The biggest roadblock … lead time on parts … dealing with RS3 parts … not a lot of these cars in wreckers" (Ep 2 5:01).
- **Status (2026):** running drag car, cage certified to 8.5 s, polycarbonate windows, carbon roof, stripped interior; 2026 first passes ~10.5–11 s with knock on old fuel; 2025 personal best not stated in these transcripts.

## What this changes in our A3 conclusions

1. **Precedent exists, in Canada.** A VR6 in an S3 8V with AWD retained, on a factory VW ECU, is running. Our earlier "no VR6 in any A3/S3/RS3 8V" was wrong; the search could not see YouTube.
2. **The gearbox answer is the RS3's DQ500 with a machined bellhousing**, not the S3's own DQ250. The engine-side mount is the Atlas VR6 mount; the trans mount and dogbone are RS3 parts. All bolt to the 8V frame rails.
3. **The ECU blocker has a vendor.** TuneZilla ran an Atlas MED17 ECU in an S3 with its own software. They sell tunes remotely (tickets.tunezilla.com) and are the company to ask for a swap calibration quote.
4. It is still a professional tuning shop's race-car build with imported European parts (BWS long block, used DQ500, RS3 rad support), weeks of wiring trial-and-error, and no published cost.
